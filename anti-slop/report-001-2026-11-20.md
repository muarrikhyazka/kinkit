## UI/UX improvements beyond the audit

- **Shorter landing page.** The 33-item feature wall is now 3 groups showing 4 to 5 key items each, with the rest behind a native `<details>` toggle ("6 more money features") that works without JS. The "Everywhere" group repeated the Screens and Download sections, so it was folded into them. Nav went from 8 links to 6.
- **Shorter hero lead**, from 4 sentences to 2.
- **Bot commands** (`/remind`, `/buy`, `/chores`, `/menu`) moved into the FAQ answer about bots.
- **Login form:**
  - The heading now says the task ("Log in" / "Create your household") instead of repeating the logo.
  - "Login" became "Log in", and "Create account" became "Create household".
  - Added `autocomplete` (`username`, `current-password` / `new-password`, `name`), so password managers work and the browser warning is gone.
  - Sign-up shows an "At least 8 characters" hint.
- **Privacy page** fixes: the header actions now align right, the footer has no empty band, and the footer divider stays within the content width.
- **Per-page titles** are now translated by the EN/ID toggle (`data-i18n-title`).
