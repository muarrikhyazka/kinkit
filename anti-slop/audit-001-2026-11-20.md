# antislop audit 001 (2026-11-20)

Mode: AFTER (audit only, nothing changed). Repo: `muarrikhyazka/kinkit` at local `main` (clean, in sync with origin).

Scope: `landing/`, `react/src/` (app and in-app docs), plus a grep pass over `mobile/`, `tv/`, `backend-go/` and `bots/` for user-facing text and comment hygiene.
Method: code inspection and the antislop contrast checker. The apps were not run in a browser, so nothing here was click-tested.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Lock = LOW.

## Findings

### HIGH

**1. Placeholder pricing reads as real (R-38, R-36, C-5)**
`landing/index.html` `#pricing`: "Rp49.000 / month", a "Best for families" label, and a Free vs Family split that puts family members, roles, bots, receipts and CSV import in the paid tier. The HTML comment says the app gates nothing by plan, so every household gets all of it today. The one-line "preview and may change" note does not undo a realistic price and a false feature split. Fix: remove the section and its nav link until pricing exists, or replace it with one honest line (for example "Free while in early access").

**2. "Undo instead of Are you sure?" claim is false (R-38, C-5)**
`landing/index.html` (`f.undo.d`) says "Every delete shows an Undo button … nobody gets stuck answering confirmation dialogs." The web app still has 7 `window.confirm` dialogs: `react/src/TVTokens.jsx:68`, `Receipts.jsx:93`, `pages/ImportExport.jsx:299`, `pages/Lists.jsx:437`, `pages/Widgets.jsx:72` and `:130`, `pages/Calendar.jsx:139`. Fix: reword to "Most deletes can be undone" or replace those confirms with undo toasts.

**3. Em dashes in user-facing text (R-02)**
- `landing/assets/og-image.svg:14`: the social card headline is "— and everyone in it."
- `backend-go/reminders_bot.go:82` and `:262`: the reminder chat messages users receive.
- `mobile/lib/screens/budget_screen.dart:249`: `'—'` used as the empty-amount glyph.

Fix: use a comma or colon in the copy, and use `-` or "not set" for the empty amount.

**4. Endless motion with no pause control (R-39)**
- The login WebGL scene loops for as long as the page is open (`react/src/LoginScene.js:372`).
- The mascot bob, blink, coin and zzz animations are `infinite` on the landing page (`landing/styles.css:435-447`) and in the app (`react/src/mascot.css`).

Both respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, which is good, but the rule also asks that anything moving longer than 5 seconds can be paused. Fix: stop after a few cycles (rest pose), or add a pause toggle.

**5. Landing tap targets under 44px (R-03)**
- `.lang-toggle button` is 36×32 (`landing/styles.css:166`).
- `.btn-sm` is 40px tall (`:129`), and it's used for the header Sign in and Start free buttons and every Download button.
- `.link-chev` is already fixed for coarse pointers (`:145`), but these two are not.

Fix: give both 44px under `(pointer: coarse)`.

**6. No DESIGN.md or declared dials (R-37)**
The direction exists, but it is spread across `react/DESIGN_AUDIT.md` and `landing/README.md`. There is no single direction file and no ENERGY/RHYTHM/MOTION dials, so future UI work has nothing to be checked against. Fix: write a short `DESIGN.md` covering identity, palette, type, the Kiki mascot as motif, and the dials, and point `CLAUDE.md` at it.

### MEDIUM

**7. No privacy policy or terms on the landing page (Part 1 "Demo Without a Product", C-5)**
The page asks families to put money, kids' data and receipts into the product and makes privacy promises in `#privacy`. The footer links to no policy or terms. Fix: add real Privacy and Terms pages, or at least a contact and data-handling page.

**8. Decorative gradients left over (R-01)**
- `react/src/pages/login.css:20-23`: two radial orbs, one of them a pink glow, over a purple gradient.
- `react/src/docs/docs.css:153`: `.dx-hero` has a radial highlight plus a linear gradient.

Both contradict the "no gradients or glows" system in `DESIGN_AUDIT.md`. The chrome gradient (`index.css:24`) is documented as brand chrome, so it is not flagged. Fix: use flat `--chrome` colour, or write down why the login screen is an exception.

**9. Eyebrow news link above the H1 (R-09)**
`landing/index.html` hero: "Levels 1 to 50, 20+ badges and Slack & Matrix bots are here" sits directly above the headline. It is a release note that will go stale, and it competes with the H1. Fix: drop it, or move it below the lead as a dated "New" line.

**10. Generic icons with no written relevance (R-04)**
- Landing: `i-zap` (lightning) for "Log spending in seconds", and `i-star` for "Levels 1 to 50".
- In-app docs: `DRocket` for "Getting started", and `DStar` for points (`react/src/docs/pages/introduction.jsx`).

Fix: swap them for content icons (a keypad or receipt, a ladder or level bar, a door or key), or record why each was chosen.

**11. Uppercase, wide-tracked labels (R-06)**
`landing/styles.css:360` (`.plan-label`) and `:410` (footer headings). Fix: use sentence case, or write the reason down.

### LOW

**12. "Up and running in three steps" with 01/02/03 (R-05)**
`landing/index.html` `#how`. The content is real, but the frame is the stock 3-step template, and "See how it works" points to it twice. Fix: show the steps as one real flow (for example a chat message that becomes a budget bar), or merge them into the features section.

**13. Template footer (R-05)**
About plus Product / Your data / Account columns. Fix: shorten it to what a visitor needs (sign in, download, docs, privacy), in one row.

**14. Generic CTA in the in-app docs (R-15)**
`react/src/docs/pages/introduction.jsx:17` says "Get started". Fix: "Create your household" or "Log in".

**15. Copy drift and stock phrases (R-16, copywriting)**
- Stock phrases: "Recurring payments on autopilot" and "Unlock … badges".
- Positioning drift: the hero says "not just its budget", but the final CTA ("Bring your family's money home"), the footer and the OG image ("One home for your family's money") go back to money-only.
- `og:image:alt` describes a different headline from the one the image actually shows.

Fix: pick one line and use it everywhere.

**16. Decorative banner comments (antislop-code)**
There are 227 comments built from `----` / `====` / box drawing. The heaviest files are `mobile/lib/api/models.dart` (17), `landing/styles.css` (16), `mobile/lib/api/api_client.dart` (13), `landing/main.js` and `landing/index.html` (11 each, including the boxed pricing banner) and `tv/web/app.js` (10). Fix: turn each one into a single plain comment, or delete it. Comments only, no code changes.

## Checked and fine

- **Contrast:** WCAG AA passes on all main landing pairs (muted 5.5:1 light / 7.1:1 dark, white on purple 6.3:1, header secondary text 5.1:1).
- **Claims:** "Levels 1 to 50", "20+ badges" (21 defined) and "10 points a day" match `backend-go/gamification.go` and `achievements.go`.
- **Content:** no fake stats, testimonials or logo bars. The FAQ is product-specific.
- **Keyboard and markup:** global `:focus-visible` rings. No clickable divs. Images have `alt`.
- **States:** every data page in the web app has loading, error and empty states (`useApi` + skeletons).
- **Motion:** `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured.
