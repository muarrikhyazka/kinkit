Features

Everything a household needs, nothing it doesn't

From the first "25k makan siang" to a year-end review, Kinkit covers money, family roles, motivation and every screen in the house.

Money

Know where it goes, and plan where it should.

Log spending in seconds

Add from any page. Type 25k, 2rb or 1.5jt. Kinkit remembers each description's category and offers your recent entries as one-tap chips.

Budgets that show reality

Plan income and spending per category, grouped as Needs, Wants and Savings. Every cell shows what you actually spent: green, then amber at 80%, red when over.

Budget alerts in your chat

Get a Telegram or Discord message when a category reaches 80% of its monthly budget, and again if it goes over.

Income: planned vs received

Record money that actually came in and compare it with the plan for the month, the year and across years.

Recurring payments on autopilot

Enter rent, subscriptions or loan payments once (monthly, weekly or yearly) and Kinkit adds each one for you. Pause and resume any time.

Savings goals and net worth

Each goal gets a progress ring and shows how much to save per month to hit its date. Track account balances and progress toward a net-worth target.

Receipts that read themselves

Snap a receipt and keep it with its transaction. "Read amount from receipt" finds the total on your device, so the photo isn't sent anywhere to be read.

Bank CSV import

Import BCA, Mandiri or any generic CSV. Preview and re-categorise rows first. Importing a file twice never creates duplicates, and any import can be undone.

Dashboards your way

Week, Month, Year and Across Years views, plus a Custom tab where you pick the charts and drag to reorder them. Hide big one-offs, then export to PDF.

Multi-currency display

See amounts in IDR, USD, EUR, GBP, SGD, MYR, JPY, AUD or INR, converted with daily exchange rates. Your stored data stays in rupiah.

Home & family

One household, everyone with the right access.

One household, everyone signs in

Add family members with their own email and password. Everyone works on the same household's money.

Roles that fit real families

Family Admin and Parent manage everything. Kids log spending and can only edit their own entries. Viewers have read-only access, which suits a grandparent or an accountant.

Kid allowances

Give each kid a monthly allowance and let them see how much they've spent so far this month.

Undo instead of "Are you sure?"

Deleted something by mistake? Every delete shows an Undo button for a few seconds, so nobody gets stuck answering confirmation dialogs.

Today

One daily view of what's due for the household: reminders, chores, lists and how the budget is doing.

Chores, reminders & shopping lists

Chores with points, parent approval and photo proof that count toward kids' allowances. Repeating reminders for bills, medicine, school and birthdays that nudge the right person on Telegram or Discord. Shared shopping lists where you type "eggs, 2 milk, bread x3", then turn the trip into one spending entry.

Fun & motivation

Good money habits, turned into a family game.

Points and levels

Earn 10 points a day for logging, plus a streak bonus. Climb from Getting Started to Tracker, Budget Buddy, Money Master and Finance Wizard.

Streaks and badges

Unlock First Log, Streak 7, Streak 30, First Budget Set, Under Budget and Saver, each with a confetti celebration.

Weekly family challenges

Log every day, have a no-spend weekend, keep Wants under a week's share of the budget. Each one adds bonus points to the household total.

Monthly leaderboard

See who earned the most points this month. A friendly race that makes everyone want to log.

A theme for every age

Light, Dark, a Playful theme for kids and a high-contrast Senior theme with bigger text. See them.

Everywhere

Web, phone, TV and the chat apps you already use.

Web app you can install

Works in any modern browser. Install it on Android, iPhone or desktop to get its own icon and a full-screen window.

Works offline

No signal at the market? Spending you add offline stays on the device and syncs as soon as you're back online, and nothing disappears silently.

TV & car display links

Open a share link on a TV browser, wall tablet or car head unit to show this month's numbers in big type. It needs no login, refreshes every minute and can be revoked instantly.

Android phone app

A native app for the whole family: Today, reminders, chores, lists, spending and budgets. Download the APK. iPhone app coming soon.

Smart TV viewer

A view-only family dashboard for Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV and LG webOS, with a Samsung Tizen package for developer mode. Get it.

FAQ

Questions families ask

Do I have to connect my bank account?

No. Kinkit never asks for bank credentials. Log spending in the app or from chat, or import the CSV statement your bank already lets you download (BCA, Mandiri or a generic format). You see a preview before anything is saved.

Can my kids use it?

Yes. Give them the Kid role. They can log their own spending, edit only their own entries and see their monthly allowance. They earn points and badges too, and the Playful theme is made for them.

Can I get my data out?

Always. Under Settings › Data & devices you can export spending (optionally for a date range) and income as CSV, or download a full JSON backup of everything.

Can I host Kinkit myself?

Yes. The repository includes a docker-compose file that runs the Go app, a Postgres database and the Telegram and Discord bots. Set a couple of secrets in .env and run docker compose up -d.

Does it work without internet?

The installed app opens offline and shows the data from your last visit. Spending you add offline is saved on the device and synced automatically when you reconnect. If the server rejects an entry, it is listed so nothing gets lost quietly.

How do the Telegram and Discord bots work?

Create your own bot (for example with BotFather on Telegram) and paste its token and chat id under Settings › Bots. Then send messages like "50k makan siang" to log spending, or /recent (!recent on Discord) to see the last entries. The same bot sends your budget alerts.

Which currencies are supported?

Amounts are stored in rupiah (IDR). You can display them in IDR, USD, EUR, GBP, SGD, MYR, JPY, AUD or INR, converted with daily exchange rates.

Is there a phone or TV app?

Today you can install the web app on Android, iPhone or desktop, and open a TV display link in any TV or car browser. A native iOS/Android app and a view-only smart TV app for Android TV / Google TV, Samsung Tizen and LG webOS are coming soon.
